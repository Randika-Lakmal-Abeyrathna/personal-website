How I work with AI

I treat AI tools as a fast pair programmer, not an authority. They speed up the reading, drafting and checking around my work; the design decisions, and the responsibility for what ships, stay with me.

Every day

  • Reading legacy code. Getting oriented in old Java and JSP modules before migrating them.
  • Drafting tests and boilerplate, then reviewing every line.
  • A second look at SQL and execution plans while tuning Oracle.
  • First-pass reviews before I open a pull request.

With my team

  • Bringing AI tools into the team's workflow, with clear ground rules.
  • Same review bar. AI-written code goes through the same review as anything else.
  • Nothing sensitive goes into a prompt: no customer data, no secrets.
  • Sharing what works. Useful prompts and patterns get passed around, not reinvented.
